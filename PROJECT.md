# Project Overview

## Name

VIALERT

## Tagline

The city responds before you arrive.

## One-Line Pitch

VIALERT creates a live emergency corridor by combining traffic-aware routing with signal-priority recommendations for verified emergency vehicles.

## Target Users

- Emergency dispatch operators
- Ambulance drivers
- Traffic control room operators
- City traffic departments
- Hospitals and emergency response coordinators

## Why This Matters

In many cities, emergency vehicles must move through unpredictable congestion. Even when a driver knows the route, delays can happen because traffic lights, accidents, and congestion are not coordinated around the emergency vehicle.

## What The MVP Proves

The MVP proves that a system can:

- model a city as a road graph,
- react to changing road conditions,
- route an ambulance around problems,
- coordinate signal priority on the selected path,
- show the full decision process in a dashboard.

## What The MVP Does Not Claim

- It does not claim real-world time savings without field testing.
- It does not directly control actual traffic signals.
- It does not replace traffic police or official traffic controllers.
- It does not use real CCTV unless added later.
